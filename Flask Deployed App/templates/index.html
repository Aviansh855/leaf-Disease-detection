Why detect plant disease?

Plant diseases affect the growth of their species. Proper identification of the disease and its cause is essential — without it, control measures can waste time and money and lead to further plant losses. Early, accurate diagnosis saves crops.

Prevent plant disease
  • Follow good sanitation practices
  • Fertilize to keep plants healthy
  • Inspect plants before bringing them home
  • Allow the soil to warm before planting
  • Rotate crops regularly
  • Provide good air circulation
  • Remove diseased stems and foliage
